## Overview

As a research assistant at the Center for Health Policy and Management (UGM), I contributed
to mapping the digital technology ecosystem used in COVID-19 response across Jakarta and
Yogyakarta.

## Objective

Understand how digital tools were used during the pandemic to inform better crisis-management
technology and data-driven policy.

## Approach

- Collected data on digital tools and actors involved in COVID-19 response
- Mapped the relationships across the ecosystem
- Synthesized findings into reports to support policy development

## Outcome

The findings were published to advance crisis-management technology and data-driven policy
making.
