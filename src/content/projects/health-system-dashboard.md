## Overview

Built during my work at the Center for Health Policy and Management (UGM), this dashboard
visualizes Indonesia's health system indicators so policymakers can explore the data and
make evidence-based decisions.

## Objective

Make complex health system data accessible and easy to interpret for non-technical
stakeholders.

## Approach

- Collected and structured health data from multiple regions
- Designed clear, interactive visualizations
- Organized the views around the questions policymakers care about

## Tech Stack

- Tableau
- Data cleaning in Python / spreadsheets

## Outcome

An interactive tool supporting health policy analysis.
