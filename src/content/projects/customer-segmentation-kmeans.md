## Overview

This analysis groups customers into segments based on their behavior and attributes, giving
the business a clearer picture of who its customers are and how to serve them.

## Objective

Discover natural customer groups to support targeted marketing and product decisions.

## Approach

- Cleaned and scaled the customer features
- Used the elbow method and silhouette scores to choose the number of clusters
- Applied K-Means and profiled each resulting segment

## Tech Stack

- Python (pandas, NumPy)
- scikit-learn
- Matplotlib / Seaborn

## Outcome

Each segment can be described and acted on with a tailored strategy.
