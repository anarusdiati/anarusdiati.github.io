## Overview

This project explores how bike-sharing demand changes across seasons, weather conditions,
and times of day, turning raw ride data into clear, actionable insights.

## Objective

Understand the drivers of bike-sharing demand to support planning and operations.

## Approach

- Cleaned and aggregated the ride data
- Explored trends by hour, day, season, and weather
- Built visualizations to communicate the patterns clearly

## Tech Stack

- Python (pandas, NumPy)
- Matplotlib / Seaborn

## Outcome

A set of visual insights into when and why demand peaks.
