## Overview

Cognivia is an AI-powered space for structuring thoughts, reducing cognitive overload, and
restoring clarity. I founded it to explore how AI can act as a **cognitive mirror** —
helping people see their thoughts more clearly and return to the present moment.

## Clarity Over Comfort

Cognivia is built on the belief that the mind should be understood, not suppressed. Clarity
emerges through structured awareness, not constant reassurance.

## The Concept

Rather than another chatbot that only soothes, Cognivia focuses on reflection and structure:
turning a tangle of thoughts into something a person can actually see, organize, and work
through — then step back into the present moment.

## Status

This project is in its early stage and actively evolving. I'm currently designing the mobile
experience and prototyping the core interactions.
