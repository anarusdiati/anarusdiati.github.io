About

What this site does

AnxietyPredictor estimates an anxiety score (1–10) from 16 daily and physiological inputs using an XGBoost model — the same model as the iOS app, running entirely in your browser via ONNX Runtime Web. It also explains the top contributing factors and offers contextual suggestions.

Privacy

Your inputs and results never leave this device — there's no server, no account, and no network request carrying your data. Prediction runs locally in your browser. Past assessments are stored only in this browser's local storage and are removed if you clear your browser data.

Model

Trained with XGBoost on the enhanced anxiety dataset (11,000 rows). Optuna-tuned hyperparameters: 496 trees, max depth 5, learning rate ≈ 0.0106. The same 16 named inputs the iOS app sends are used here, exported to ONNX instead of CoreML.

How accurate is it?

77%of score variation explained (R²)

In plain terms: if your true score should be a 6, the model usually lands somewhere around 5–7. That's not the model being sloppy — roughly the remaining 23% comes from things these 16 numbers can't see, like your personality, life circumstances, or how you're feeling in ways a form can't fully capture. Treat the result as a reasonably informed estimate, not an exact reading.
